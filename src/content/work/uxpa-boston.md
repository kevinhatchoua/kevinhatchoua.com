## Context

[UXPA Boston](https://uxpaboston.org/) is where practitioners learn, get inspired, and support one another through conferences, UX Fair, speaker series, and social events.

## Approach

As a board member, I help shape programming that lowers barriers for emerging designers while keeping the chapter sustainable for volunteers and sponsors.

## Process

- Support for the annual conference (2026 home: Revere Hotel Boston Common, May 8).
- Blind-reviewed speaker pipeline and community partnerships.
- Advocacy for mentorship, accessibility, and digital inclusion in chapter programming.

Public bio highlights: volunteering with Virtual Activism and AfriHope, BU Spark mentorship, and a focus on how design and technology create real community impact.

## Outcome

More welcoming, predictable chapter experiences for members across career stages.

## Reflection

Community design is service design. The audience is your neighbor.
