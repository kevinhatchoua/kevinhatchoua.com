## Context

SeaChange needed consistent, modern experiences across Adrenaline and AssetFlow while simplifying complex navigation.

## Approach

Led UX and UI development: analytics-informed redesign, unified style guide, and streamlined IA across apps.

## Process

- Heuristic review and analytics synthesis (Appsee, Hotjar, and related tools noted on legacy portfolio).
- Axure prototypes and Confluence documentation.
- HTML/CSS/Bootstrap implementation partnership with engineering.

![SeaChange redesign](/images/work/seachange.jpg)

## Outcome

Stronger cross-app consistency and a style guide that governed future releases.

## Reflection

Legacy CMS UX is archaeology. You earn trust by respecting expert users while fixing sharp edges.
