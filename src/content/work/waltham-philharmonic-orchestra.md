## Context

The Waltham Philharmonic Orchestra ran a graphic design contest. I was a finalist with this promotional poster for concert promotion online and in print.

## Approach

Illustration, typography, and photo work in Photoshop and Illustrator. The poster had to read at a distance and still hold up as a social graphic.

## Process

- Composed the hero illustration and orchestral type hierarchy.
- Prepared a single layout for both print and digital crops.


## Outcome

Finalist entry in the orchestra’s open call. The piece lives in my Behance archive from that year.

## Reflection

Community arts orgs deserve the same craft as big clients. The deadline was real even without a corporate brief.
