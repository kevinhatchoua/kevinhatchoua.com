## Context

Cuvilly needed a welcoming digital home for programs, enrollment, and community engagement.

## Approach

Built IA around programs, projects, and community. Paired the site with an active social presence.

## Process

- Wireframes and content hierarchy for parents and educators.
- Visual design reflecting ecological mission.
- SEO foundations for an established local institution.

![Cuvilly website](/images/work/cuvilly.png)

## Outcome

Clear paths to program information and forms without relying on phone-only inquiries.

## Reflection

Small nonprofits deserve big craft. Clarity is kindness.
