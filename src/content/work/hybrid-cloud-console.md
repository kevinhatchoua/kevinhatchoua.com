## Context

Operators needed a coherent entry point to hybrid cloud capabilities instead of a patchwork of product silos. Console users span day-one adopters and experienced operators; getting started, onboarding, and ongoing navigation had to work for both.

## Approach

I worked with researchers and fellow designers to improve getting started and onboarding, and to evolve platform navigation, information architecture, and logical grouping of services across the console.

We facilitated discovery, audited existing journeys, aligned on IA, and prototyped navigation models that scale as new services land. Content strategy and in-product guidance were part of the same story—not a layer added at the end.

## Process

- Journey mapping and service blueprinting across console surfaces.
- Content strategy for in-product guidance and cross-linking.
- Iterative wireframes validated with internal stakeholders and open source contributors.

![Console exploration](/images/work/hybrid-cloud-console.png)

## Outcome

Clearer narrative for how users discover, launch, and manage hybrid cloud capabilities from a single console frame, with measurable emphasis on successful first sessions and continued adoption.

## Reflection

Hybrid cloud UX is an exercise in translation: the same mental model must work for a seasoned SRE and a developer on day one—and analytics should tell us when it does not.
