## Context

The November 2015 Paris attacks were a series of coordinated terrorist attacks that took place on Friday, 13 November 2015 in Paris, France and the city's northern suburb, Saint-Denis. Three suicide bombers struck outside the Stade de France in Saint-Denis, during a football match. This was followed by several mass shootings and a suicide bombing, at cafés and restaurants. Gunmen carried out another mass shooting and took hostages at an Eagles of Death Metal concert in the Bataclan theatre, leading to a stand-off with police. The attackers were shot or blew themselves up when police raided the theater.

In the wake of the attacks, I completed this project as a dedication to the beauty and resiliency of the city and the French people. Vive la France!

## Approach

Minimal line work, a fixed Eiffel Tower anchor, and layered motion (clouds, sun, moon, birds, city, and fireworks) to move from daylight into night—a quiet day-to-night cycle rather than spectacle for its own sake.

## Process

### Storyboard

Detailing element placement and movement across the full frame: the tower stays on screen while clouds disperse, the sun exits as darkness sets, the background gradient shifts from light to dark, birds launch on marked paths, and the moon rises at the end.

![Storyboard sketch mapping motion across the Paris skyline, with notes for clouds, sun, gradient, birds, and moon](/images/work/la-parisienne/storyboard-motion.png)

*Storyboard: placement, pacing, and motion notes for the day-to-night transition.*

### Thumbnails

Detailing how each motif reads in a minimal style—birds without extra detail, an oversized textured moon, an old-style airplane, a dark city outline with the tower in depth, soft feathered clouds, and fireworks built from short segments that appear in sequence.

![Thumbnail grid for birds, moon, airplane, city, clouds, and fireworks](/images/work/la-parisienne/storyboard-thumbnails.png)

*Thumbnails: art direction for each moving element before animation.*

## Reflection

Paper first. When the storyboard carries the emotion, the animation has somewhere honest to go.
