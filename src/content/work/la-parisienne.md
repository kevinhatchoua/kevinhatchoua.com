## Context

A narrative animation needed character exploration and sequential art direction.

## Approach

Storyboards, illustration frames, and motion planning for a hand-crafted look.

## Process

- Thumbnail storyboards and pacing studies.
- Illustration of key frames and color scripts.

![La Parisienne frames](/images/work/la-parisienne.png)

## Outcome

A visual spine the animation team could produce against.

## Reflection

Motion starts on paper. If the storyboard bores you, fix it before pixels.
