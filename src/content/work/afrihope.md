## Context

AfriHope strengthens marginalized communities through information and communication technologies. I serve on the board alongside my design contributions.

## Approach

Clarity-first UX for mission, programs, and calls to support. The goal was trust: visitors should understand who AfriHope serves and how to help within seconds.

## Process

- Information architecture for programs and donation paths.
- Visual design aligned to community leadership and accessibility.
- Ongoing board advocacy for digital inclusion and equal rights.

![AfriHope brand application](/images/work/apex-predator.jpg)

## Outcome

A maintainable web presence organizers could share with partners, paired with governance work that keeps digital inclusion central to the mission.

## Reflection

Nonprofit UX is stewardship. The site should feel as caring as the people behind it.
