## Context

Product teams needed faster ways to align on layout and component usage before high-fidelity design.

## Approach

Authored wireframe patterns, annotated behaviors, and content notes tied to PatternFly components. Partnered with design system maintainers so wireframes stayed implementable.

## Process

- Inventory of recurring console patterns (lists, details, wizards, dashboards).
- Library structure with usage guidance and accessibility callouts.
- Feedback loops with designers embedded in product squads.

![Wireframe library](/images/work/patternfly-wireframe-library.webp)

## Outcome

Shorter time from problem framing to review-ready concepts, with fewer one-off layout surprises in build.

## Reflection

Wireframes are contracts. The library made those contracts reusable.
