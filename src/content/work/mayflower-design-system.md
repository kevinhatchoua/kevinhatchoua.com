## Client

The Executive Office of Technology Services and Security (EOTSS) places our customers and constituents at the heart of everything we do. We offer responsive digital services and productivity tools to more than 40,000 state employees, who provide essential information and services to the citizens of the Commonwealth.

We also serve constituents, providing them with digital services and tools that enable taxpayers, motorists, businesses, visitors, families, and other citizens to do business with the Commonwealth in a way that makes every interaction with government easier, faster, and more secure.

## Project

I collaborated with developers and project managers to establish a project roadmap that helped us define and establish the pillars of the Mass.gov brand. This design system includes various products such as the Mass.gov style guide, Mayflower React component library, Mayflower Pattern Lab, and the Mayflower system GitBook documentation.

I was primarily responsible for leading the design and implementation of the Mass.gov Style Guide. The information contained in this document is the product of our research, experience, and expertise, collected in one place to help detail and explain our decisions in creating Mass.gov.

## Challenges

- Multiple agencies rely on our code and designs to support their apps.
- Defining the Mass.gov brand within the greater Commonwealth brand.
- Resources, assets, and documentation live in separate environments.

## Solution

Our team designed and developed a scalable design system (the Mayflower Design System). This is a collection of modular components, color theory, design principles, and documentation that support and guide our design process. Mayflower makes it easier to build accessible, mobile-friendly websites with a consistent look and feel for the state of Massachusetts.

### Mass.gov Style Guide

This guide is an outline of the elements and strategies that make up the Mass.gov brand identity. Refer to it in order to help you create print or digital assets that will feel like they are in a similar style to Mass.gov and belong to the Commonwealth’s information ecosystem.

## Reflection

Government design systems are public infrastructure. When brand, code, and documentation align, constituents get experiences that feel official, understandable, and trustworthy.
