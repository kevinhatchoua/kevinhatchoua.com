## Context

HRD sought design directions that made workforce demographics easier to understand while meeting accessibility expectations.

## Approach

Collaborated on visual hierarchy, chart choices, and wireframes reviewed with HRD and the Governor's office.

## Process

- Accessibility-minded comps in Illustrator and Photoshop.
- JavaScript wireframe experiments noted on legacy portfolio.
- Feedback cycles on attention and clarity without sensationalizing data.

## Outcome

Actionable design guidance for a more legible public dashboard.

## Reflection

When data is about people, tone matters as much as typography.
