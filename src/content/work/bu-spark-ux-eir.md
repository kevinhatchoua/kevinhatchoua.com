## Context

[BU Spark!](https://www.bu.edu/spark/) is an innovation and experiential learning lab in Boston University's Faculty of Computing & Data Sciences. The Experts in Residence program pairs industry practitioners with student teams on real projects.

## Approach

I mentor design students and cross-functional Spark teams to create user-centered experiences, advocate for UX practices that support agile delivery, and facilitate stakeholder reviews.

## Process

- Studio coaching on problem framing, research synthesis, and prototype storytelling.
- Partnership with faculty and partners on information architecture and decision-ready artifacts.
- Support for initiatives that expand opportunity and reduce disparities (including community-focused student projects).

Public program bio notes my work at Red Hat on discover-learn-try-onboard journeys, PatternFly contributions, and nonprofit board service with Virtual Activism and AfriHope.

## Outcome

Teams ship sharper narratives and more testable concepts within semester timelines.

## Reflection

Teaching founders and students to show their thinking is as important as polishing pixels.
