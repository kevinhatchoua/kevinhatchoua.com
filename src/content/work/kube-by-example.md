## Context

Learners needed approachable, example-driven paths into Kubernetes concepts without assuming a classroom environment. Kube by Example (KBE) aims to offer broad, practical Kubernetes tutorials, news, and community interaction in one place.

## Approach

Each lesson follows a learn-by-doing model: readers absorb concepts by practicing skills as they are explained, whether they are operators or developers building cluster literacy.

I collaborated on information architecture, lesson templates, and CMS-friendly content patterns that scale as new courses ship. Navigation connects examples, labs, and related resources so progress feels continuous rather than episodic.

## Process

- Content modeling for lessons, labs, and supporting media.
- UX for navigation between examples and related resources.
- Accessibility and readability passes on long-form instructional pages.

![Kube by Example](/images/work/kube-by-example.png)

## Outcome

A durable template system for new lessons and a clearer learning loop from reading to doing, aligned with how practitioners actually pick up Kubernetes skills.

## Reflection

Teaching UX is still UX: respect cognitive load, celebrate small wins, and measure whether people finish a lesson feeling capable—not just informed.
