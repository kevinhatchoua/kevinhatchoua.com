## Context

Students entering UX need structured feedback, portfolio guidance, and honest industry context.

## Approach

Office hours combine portfolio reviews, interview practice, and craft coaching tuned to each student's goals.

## Process

- Repeatable critique framework (clarity, evidence, accessibility, storytelling).
- Templates for case study structure and presentation.
- Ongoing relationship building with faculty and career services.

## Outcome

Students leave sessions with actionable next steps, not vague encouragement.

## Reflection

Mentorship is design work on people. Listen longer than you lecture.
