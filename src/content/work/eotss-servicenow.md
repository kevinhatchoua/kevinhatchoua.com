## Context

EOTSS migrated asset and service data into ServiceNow while keeping ITIL practitioners productive.

## Approach

Served as SME for hardware/software lifecycle workflows. Partnered with developers on forms, views, and portal pages; produced enablement documentation for mixed skill levels.

## Process

- Requirements from auditors and internal teams (public portfolio describes reconciliation work at a high level).
- Portal IA and form design in ServiceNow.
- Tips-and-tricks guides to soften the application transition.

![ServiceNow portal work](/images/work/eotss-servicenow.png)

## Outcome

More usable ServiceNow surfaces and clearer guidance for everyday ITIL users during migration.

## Reflection

Enterprise migrations are UX projects. The UI is where trust is won or lost.
