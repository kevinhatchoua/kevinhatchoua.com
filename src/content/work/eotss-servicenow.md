## Client

EOTSS places our customers and constituents at the heart of everything we do. We offer responsive digital services and productivity tools to more than 40,000 state employees, who provide essential information and services to the citizens of the Commonwealth.

We also serve constituents, providing them with digital services and tools that enable taxpayers, motorists, businesses, visitors, families, and other citizens to do business with the Commonwealth in a way that makes every interaction with government easier, faster, and more secure.

## Project

My role on this project was to act as the subject matter expert and manage the life cycle for all of the government owned hardware and software assets. I worked with outside auditors (KPMG) and internal teams on the data migration from IBM systems to ServiceNow. I also worked alongside ServiceNow developers to gather requirements from stakeholders necessary to design forms, views, and portal pages.

Additionally, I created user-friendly documentation on ServiceNow tips and tricks to facilitate the transition between applications for seasoned ITIL and everyday users.

## Challenges

- Data discrepancies.
- Inability to integrate the legacy IBM system with ServiceNow.
- Extensive amount of data to be reconciled manually.
- Missing financial and contractual asset data.

## Tools & technology

- IBM Maximo
- ServiceNow
- IBM Tivoli
- Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Word, PowerPoint), Photoshop, Illustrator
- HTML, JavaScript, CSS, SQL

## Solution

EOTSS completed a comprehensive inventory of capital fixed assets including software, hardware, and non-IT holdings. This several-month undertaking, which involved contributions from Finance, Operations, Facilities, and Procurement, represents the first comprehensive capital fixed asset inventory completed by MassIT/EOTSS staff in recent memory.

We deployed several changes for our Incident management, Asset management, Change management, Problem management, Service catalog, Service portal, and Procurement applications to meet the needs of the organisation and external clients. We then migrated the results from our reconciliation and mapped all records to ServiceNow configurations.

Reconcile and migrate asset records between three sources (ServiceNow Discovery, KPMG audit, and finance reports), using key data points such as model, cost, version, components, status, depreciation, and contracts to analyze and determine valuable data.
