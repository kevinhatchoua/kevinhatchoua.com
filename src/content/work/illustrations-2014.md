## Context

A grab bag of illustration work I posted to Behance in 2014: figurative pieces, some 3D rendering, and package-design studies from a few years earlier.

## Approach

Mostly personal practice between classes. Mix of hand-drawn finishes and rendered comps.

## Process

- Illustrated scenes and characters at a time when I was still finding a line style.
- Explored packaging and 3D form when briefs did not assign it.

Eleven pieces in one Behance gallery. Some of the line work is rough; that is the point of keeping it.

## Reflection
