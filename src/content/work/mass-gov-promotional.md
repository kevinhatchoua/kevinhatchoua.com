## Context

Agency partners needed marketing-grade landing pages beyond rigid content types. Campaigns required immersive visuals and reorderable rows.

## Approach

Designed a stackable row model with reusable components, strong calls to action, and theme variants for light-on-dark and dark-on-light photography.

## Process

- Requirements workshops with Digital Services and partner agencies.
- High-fidelity comps in Drupal 8 component language.
- Front-end collaboration (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React) for responsive behavior.

![Promotional page type](/images/work/mass-gov-promotional.png)

## Outcome

A conversion-oriented page type agencies could assemble without bespoke engineering for every campaign.

## Reflection

Flexibility without chaos means tight component rules and fearless editors.
