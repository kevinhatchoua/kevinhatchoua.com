## Context

A class assignment: build a brand for **Apex Predator**, a made-up mountain sports apparel company, and apply it across print and digital touchpoints.

## Approach

Research the category, lock a mark and type treatment, then stress-test the system on packaging, stationery, and a simple web home.

## Process

- Logo, color, and typography for a snowboard / ski audience.
- Print: labels, tags, business cards, and collateral.
- Digital: layout explorations for a product-forward site.
