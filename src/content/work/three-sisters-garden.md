## Context

A community garden collective needed to share mission, events, and volunteer opportunities online.

## Approach

Designed and built a lightweight site with testing on key conversion paths.

## Process

- Information architecture for programs and seasonal content.
- Front-end design and A/B learning on calls to action.

![Three Sisters Garden](/images/work/three-sisters-garden.png)

## Outcome

A durable web presence that supported outreach beyond word of mouth.

## Reflection

Hyperlocal projects teach humility: design serves soil and neighbors, not portfolios.
