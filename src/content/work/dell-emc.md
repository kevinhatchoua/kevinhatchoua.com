## Context

Multiple operations groups needed internal sites with shared structure but group-specific content.

## Approach

Created template sitemaps and content types, detailed wireframes for engineering, and collaborated with Ruby developers on the innovation roadmap experience.

## Process

- Global IA template adapted per group.
- Interactive wireframes for remote engineering partners.
- JIVE CMS constraints shaped component decisions.

![Dell EMC wireframes](/images/work/dell-emc.jpg)

## Outcome

Repeatable site architecture and UI specs that accelerated development across groups.

## Reflection

Distributed teams need obsessive documentation. Wireframes are the timezone bridge.
