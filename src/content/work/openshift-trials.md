## Context

Prospective users evaluating OpenShift needed clarity on what a trial includes, how long it lasts, and what happens when they convert to a paid subscription.

## Approach

Mapped trial states, error paths, and upgrade moments. Paired UX with content strategy so expectations were set before users clicked deploy.

## Process

- Flow diagrams for trial start, active use, and subscription transitions.
- Wireframes for empty states, limits, and upgrade prompts.
- Research readouts synthesized into prioritized UX backlog items.

![Trial flows](/images/work/openshift-trials.png)

## Outcome

More legible trial journey with fewer dead-end moments at activation and upgrade.

## Reflection

Trials are a promise. Design has to spell out the promise in plain language.
