## Context

The Commonwealth sought a public view of workforce demographics to support transparency goals.

## Approach

Partnered with HRD and the Governor's office on IA, chart selection, and accessible presentation of sensitive aggregate data.

## Process

- Research into dashboard legibility and critique of prior visuals.
- Wireframes balancing attention-grabbing layout with WCAG expectations.
- Iteration in Tableau and design tools with stakeholder reviews.

![Employee dashboard](/images/work/executive-branch-dashboard.png)

## Outcome

Design recommendations that made trends easier to read while meeting accessibility requirements.

## Reflection

Public data visuals carry ethical weight: show truth, avoid spectacle.
