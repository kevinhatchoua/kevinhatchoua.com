## Context

A local pub needed digital promotion for events and menus.

## Approach

Front-end design, wireframes, and email campaigns grounded in neighborhood tone.

## Process

- Site IA for hours, menus, and events.
- Email templates for recurring promotions.

![Kennedy's Pub](/images/work/kennedys-pub.jpg)

## Outcome

Consistent digital touchpoints that matched the room's personality.

## Reflection

Hospitality design should feel like a welcome, not a billboard.
