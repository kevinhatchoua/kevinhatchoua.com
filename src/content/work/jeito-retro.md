## Context

A retro fashion retailer needed online presence matching in-store personality.

## Approach

Wireframes, market research, and front-end design emphasizing product storytelling.

## Process

- IA for collections and lookbooks.
- Visual design with period-appropriate typography nods.

![Jeito Retro](/images/work/jeito-retro.png)

## Outcome

A browsable catalog experience aligned with the shop's voice.

## Reflection

Retail UX is theater. Pace the reveal like a good window display.
