## Context

A personal training brand needed a credible web presence and content structure for services.

## Approach

Market research, IA, and front-end design to support storytelling and lead capture.

## Process

- Content plan aligned to client services.
- Responsive layouts and visual language for health and movement.

![Fit with Nina](/images/work/fit-with-nina.jpg)

## Outcome

A site that matched the coach's voice and made offerings easy to scan.

## Reflection

Fitness brands live on motivation. Design should feel energetic, not noisy.
