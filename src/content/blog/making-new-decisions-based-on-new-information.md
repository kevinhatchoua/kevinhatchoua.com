Adaptation is crucial for success in a constantly changing world. As circumstances and contexts change and evolve, we must adjust our decisions and course of action accordingly.

![Person at a crossroads deciding which path to take](/images/blog/making-new-decisions.png)

"Be water, my friend." - Bruce Lee.

Flexibility requires a willingness to let go of preconceived notions and embrace new ideas and approaches. It requires a mindset of experimentation, risk-taking, and learning. A space where we are constantly seeking to innovate and improve.

In the context of UX, adaptation means designing products and experiences that can stand the test of time. It means being responsive to user feedback and continuously refining our designs and ideas to meet users' changing needs, desires, and expectations.

A notable company that failed to adopt this notion was Blockbuster. At its peak in the early 2000s, Blockbuster was a well-known household name for movies and video game rentals. As technology advanced and new players entered the market (Netflix and Hulu), Blockbuster failed to adapt to changing consumer behavior. With the rise of streaming services, consumers' preferences shifted to the convenience of online streaming rather than visiting a physical store. Blockbuster disregarded this shift in consumer behavior as a non-threat and stuck to its traditional business model.

The rest is history.

This example highlights the importance of adapting and making new decisions based on new information. Blockbuster failed to recognize the changing needs and preferences of its customers, and as a result, it became irrelevant in a rapidly evolving market.

Hindsight is 20/20, Foresight is not. Ultimately, the sweet spot is striking a balance between continuous discovery and adaptation. By staying open to new information and being willing to adapt, we can make informed decisions that lead to better user experiences and long-term success.
