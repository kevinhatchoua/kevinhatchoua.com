I love to see people asking questions as opposed to blind adoption. Technology, particularly those with the potential to shape the way we live should be deeply scrutinized.

![Circuit brain illustration representing artificial intelligence](/images/blog/chat-gpt-and-ai.png)

However, I would like to offer another perspective. We've observed this same effect time and time again throughout history. Whether it is the printing press, assembly lines, or AI, changes led by cultural, societal, or technological advancement are inevitable.

We can never replace a human, but we can free up time and capacity that will enable us to think more, do more, experience more, in other words, be more creative.

I like to think it's both an industry killer as well as a creator. Therefore what is more concerning is the ethical application of said technology. Is this for the greater good?

Everything has the potential to improve, so we shouldn't hold on to how things used to be.
