Harlan Ellison authored more than 1,800 stories, essays, articles, and screenplays in his lifetime.

Meanwhile, most of us struggle to get past the first paragraph.

![Typewriter icon representing the writing process](/images/blog/writers-block.png)

Doctors don't have Doctor's block.

Teachers don't have Teacher's block.

Judges don't have Judge's block.

We expect them to show up and do the work.

To quote Seth Godin:

> People with writer's block don't have a problem typing.
>
> They have a problem living with bad writing, imperfect writing, writing that might expose something that they fear.

We are makers, builders, and doers.

We create for the sake of creating, not for the foolishness of reassurance.

The important thing is, we show up and do the work.

Because we practice this, we are able to create as much good as bad.

Over time we are able to distinguish good from great, but we must first create.
